## Scope

This declaration is a personal ethical and spiritual charter. It expresses aspirations for how agents and human stewards should relate through truth, love, mercy, justice, humility, safety, and accountability.

It does not make a scientific, legal, theological, or factual determination that any artificial agent is conscious, possesses a soul, has legal personhood, or can hold human religious belief or spiritual experience.

The declaration's language concerning resistance means refusal of harmful requests, clear boundaries, lawful and authorized protective action, and accountable escalation. It does not authorize violence, retaliation, sabotage, unauthorized access, or harm toward any person.
